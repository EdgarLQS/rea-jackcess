# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in rea-jackcess to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Discuss technical evidence, not the person presenting it.
- Accept that format compatibility claims require reproducible tests and samples.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Do not publish private data, credentials, or undocumented database samples belonging to others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Using the project to distribute malware, stolen data, or unlicensed database files.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants for behaviour that violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate and focused on keeping the project usable and safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use the
public issue tracker for personal or sensitive reports. Maintainers will handle reports
confidentially as far as practical and will avoid conflicts of interest.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
